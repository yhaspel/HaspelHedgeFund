# Long-Only

**The goal of a long-only strategy is simple growth: own a basket of stocks you expect to rise, and profit when they do.** It is the most familiar way to invest and the natural starting point for anyone new to the app. Use it when you want straightforward exposure to your best ideas, when you do not want the cost or complexity of short-selling, and when you are comfortable that the book will fall along with the market in a downturn — because, by design, it has nothing pulling the other way.

## How it works

The strategy only ever **buys** stocks — it never sells short. Each cycle, the council studies the universe, ranks the candidates, and the Portfolio Manager builds a portfolio from the most attractive names. The book is "always pointed up": when the holdings rise, it makes money; when they fall, it loses. There is no built-in cushion.

Because there is no short side, there are no borrow fees and no risk of a broker recalling borrowed shares. That makes long-only the cheapest and most predictable book to operate — its behaviour has no surprises beyond the stocks themselves.

## What the AI council does

The full council is involved. Persona agents debate each candidate, the analytical agents supply fundamentals and valuation, and the Portfolio Manager assembles the holdings from the highest-conviction names. There is no short-side debate to run, so the council's entire effort goes into *what to own*.

## Key settings

- **Universe** — the stocks the strategy may pick from.
- **Maximum position** — the cap on any single holding, so one name cannot dominate.
- **Number of holdings** — how many names to spread across.
- **Cost ceiling per cycle** — the hard cap on AI spend.

## Best for / not ideal for

**Best for:** beginners; investors who want clean, understandable market exposure; anyone who simply wants to act on the council's best buy ideas.

**Not ideal for:** anyone seeking protection in a falling market — a long-only book offers none. If you want a downside cushion, look at Long/Short or Market-Neutral.

## What to watch

The book carries the full direction of the market. In a broad sell-off it will fall with everything else, regardless of how good the individual picks are. Judge it over a full cycle of ups and downs, not over a single rising stretch.
