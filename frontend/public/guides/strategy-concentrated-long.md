# Concentrated Long-Only

**The goal of a concentrated long-only strategy is to put real weight behind a small number of high-conviction ideas, in the style of a "best ideas" or activist investor.** Use it when you would rather own a handful of companies you understand deeply than spread thinly across dozens — and when you want the strategy to hold cash rather than dilute the book with a mediocre pick. It is for the investor who believes that a few great decisions, sized to matter, beat many average ones.

## How it works

The strategy buys stocks only — never shorts — but unlike ordinary Long-Only it holds **few names, each large**: typically 5 to 15 positions, each a meaningful 5–25% of the book. Concentration is the entire point: when an idea is right, it moves the whole portfolio.

It also enforces a **conviction bar**. If fewer ideas clear the high-confidence threshold than the minimum you set, the strategy does **not** reach for a weak pick to fill the slot — it simply holds **cash**. Discipline over activity.

## What the AI council does

The council debates each candidate deeply — concentration means every pick must withstand real scrutiny. Confidence scores carry more weight here than in any other strategy, because they decide both *what* is held and *whether a slot stays empty*. The per-position thesis is surfaced prominently, so you can read and own the reasoning behind each large bet.

## Key settings

- **Universe** — the stocks the strategy may pick from.
- **Number of holdings** — the small target count (e.g. 5–15).
- **Position size range** — the minimum and maximum weight per name.
- **Minimum holdings / conviction threshold** — how many high-confidence ideas are required before the book is fully invested; below it, the rest stays in cash.
- **Cost ceiling per cycle** — the hard cap on AI spend.

## Best for / not ideal for

**Best for:** investors who want to deeply understand and feel ownership of each holding; those who trust a focused set of strong ideas over broad diversification.

**Not ideal for:** users who want smooth, diversified returns. Concentration means one bad call is felt sharply — results are more idiosyncratic and bumpier by nature.

## What to watch

A concentrated book lives and dies by a few decisions, so its results swing more than a diversified one's. Periods of cash holding are a feature, not a fault — they mean the conviction bar is doing its job. Judge the strategy on the quality of its few bets, not on how fully invested it stays.
